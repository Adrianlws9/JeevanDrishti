4. Results & Limitations Governance Panel

  • No Empirical Efficacy Fabrication: This simulator does NOT claim that vector control, awareness campaigns, or testing will empirically reduce cases by a fixed percentage. All percentage reductions shown above are the mathematical result of the user-selected dampening sliders.
  • Strict Separation of Records: Simulated weekly counts are never written back to or merged with verified 2023–2025 IDSP outbreak surveillance records.
  • Resource Capacity Caveat: Because no hospital bed or medicine stock inventory exists in the project workspace, bed surplus/deficit figures compare hypothetical demand against the user-entered bed allocation input.
  • Non-Clinical Decision Support: For exploratory public-health scenario planning only; never replaces epidemiological field investigation or clinical judgement.
